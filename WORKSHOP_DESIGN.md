# Workshop design contract

Workshops are optional applied builds. They deepen a finished course path; they do not
extend it with another implied mandatory lesson number.

## Required shape

1. **Outcome** — name the observable behaviour or reusable tool the learner will finish.
2. **Retrieval gate** — ask the learner to recall the earlier invariant needed for the build.
3. **Deliberate bug** — show one plausible failure, make its symptom visible, and explain the
   illegal writer or broken contract.
4. **Build stations** — group closely-related field notes into two to three increasing steps.
5. **Transfer rep** — require a bounded Unity change with visual or testable acceptance criteria.
6. **Evidence** — record the real rep or review result before choosing another workshop.

## Boundaries

- A quiz is retrieval practice, not evidence of workshop completion.
- A field note may teach one rule, but it must not claim to be the required next step.
- Reuse shared browser components from `assets/` when a workshop needs a second interactive lab.
- Keep the course’s ownership vocabulary intact: locomotion/body resolves pose, gait commits
  plants and targets, support adapters evaluate committed relative state, solvers resolve pose,
  and presentation is read-only with respect to simulation.
