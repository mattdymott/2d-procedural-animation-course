Optional workshops

Choose by the outcome your project needs. Each workshop begins with retrieval, makes a deliberate failure visible, asks for a real Unity rep, and links its field notes rather than pretending every note is a mandatory next step.

1

Creature on Moving Worlds

Elevators, conveyors, turntables, and contact-point liftoff carry.

2

Port a Creature to Top-Down

Planar plants, legal footholds, turning, and one complete top-down tick.

3

Design an Insect Gait

Tripods, wave cadence, safe switching, and explicit recovery.

4

Make Motion Readable

Read-only bank, anticipation, and weight shift with a simulation A/B proof.

5

Debug a Gait Contract

Expose plant dragging and target chasing, then trace the illegal writer.
