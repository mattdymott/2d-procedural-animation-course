Creature on Moving Worlds

Make one lizard keep a real foot promise while its support rises, carries material, and rotates. The finish is not three facts remembered; it is a creature that does not skate, target-chase, or launch from the wrong velocity.

Outcome

You can decide when a world point is enough, represent a support-relative plant when it is not, and draw the point-level velocity inherited at liftoff. This extends the L9 creature tick without giving transform or presentation code ownership of gait decisions.

Retrieval gate

Before opening the lab, predict the answer: while a foot is planted on a moving support, what may change every tick, and what must not? The support pose may change; the committed support relation and local plant must not. If that distinction is not immediate, revisit the moving-platform note before continuing.

One creature, three moving-world stations

1

Ride an elevator

Replace a world plant with Support + LocalPlant. Move the lift and prove that only the derived world target changes.

2

Walk a conveyor

Keep the contact relation, but integrate SurfaceOffset in support space. The belt moves material through the platform’s coordinates; it is not a fresh terrain query.

3

Leave a turntable

Release at a rim point and carry the velocity at that contact point. A stationary support root does not mean a stationary planted foot.

Lab — make the promise visible

Start with the broken world-point representation, change the support, then switch to the relative representation. On the turntable, press Release foot and inspect the gold carry arrow.

☐ Challenge 1 — Repair the promise. On the elevator, use the correct stored state and move time past 50%.
☐ Challenge 2 — Read contact-point carry. On the turntable, preserve the relative plant and release the foot.

Implementation recipe

State boundary
// Landing is one gait decision.
leg.Support    = hit.Entity;
leg.LocalPlant = supportPose.InverseTransformPoint(hit.Point);
leg.SurfaceOffset = float2.zero;

// While planted, the adapter evaluates that decision.
leg.WorldPlant = supportPose.TransformPoint(leg.LocalPlant + leg.SurfaceOffset);
leg.SurfaceOffset += supportPose.LocalBeltVelocity * deltaTime;

// On liftoff, gait clears the relation and receives point-level carry.
carry = supportPose.PointVelocity(leg.WorldPlant)
      + supportPose.TransformDirection(supportPose.LocalBeltVelocity);
leg.Support = Entity.Null;

Do not fold the adapter into gait. Gait decides plant, swing, and liftoff; the adapter evaluates the committed relation; IK consumes the resulting world target; presentation only shows the solved result.

Transfer rep in Unity

  1. Put one L9 lizard on a sinusoidal elevator, a conveyor, and a turntable.
  2. Draw LocalPlant, WorldPlant, and the liftoff carry arrow.
  3. Toggle a deliberately frozen-world-plant bug; the foot must visibly strain or skate.
  4. Record that the correct implementation replaces a plant only at a landing transition and uses the contact point—not the support root—for rotational carry.
