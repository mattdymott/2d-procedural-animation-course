Optional workshop · planar locomotion · about 2 hours

Port a Creature to Top-Down

Port the creature’s gait contract to an XY or XZ plane without turning planted feet into body-local decorations.

Outcome

A four-legged creature turns around an obstacle, chooses one legal planar landing point, and completes the swing without retargeting. Its lift and shadow make the result readable but never decide it.

Retrieval gate

Sketch the old side-view home and its top-down replacement before reading ahead. You should retain the committed plant, comfort radius, lead, and swing; only the ground-frame shortcut changes.

1

Replace the frame

Derive heading-rotated homes from authored forward/lateral offsets. Turn in place until a persistent world plant earns honest stress.

2

Reserve a legal point

Query candidates at a permitted swing transition, filter for path/space/reach, and carry one selected world point through the swing.

3

Finish a readable tick

Layer lift and sorting over the solved planar trajectory, then add moving support only after the plant contract is sound.

Transfer rep in Unity

  1. Rotate the creature 90 degrees without translating it; draw homes, plants, and stress.
  2. Place a blocked island across one predicted home; display rejected candidates and exactly one reserved target.
  3. Turn during swing; the target must remain fixed until a landing or explicit recovery.
  4. Disable lift and shadow: the physical plant/candidate trace must remain identical.
