Optional workshop · presentation layer · about 90 minutes

Make Motion Readable

Give a solved top-down creature anticipation, bank, and weight without allowing the picture to write back into the simulation.

Outcome

An A/B toggle changes the creature’s personality while preserving its body path, plants, footholds, cadence, collision, and stop frame exactly.

Retrieval gate

List the values presentation may read and the values it must never write. If plant, target, cadence, collision, or resolved body pose appears on the write side, stop and fix the ownership boundary first.

1

Bank after a turn

Filter a render-only bank from resolved heading and turn rate.

2

Tell before the turn

Use requested turn direction for a short opposite anticipation, then hand visual control back to resolved motion.

3

Give stops weight

Filter render-only shift from resolved acceleration without extending the actual stop.

Transfer rep in Unity

  1. Record a deterministic turn-and-brake input trace.
  2. Run it with visual effects on and off; assert identical simulation plants, candidate sequence, body path, and collision result.
  3. Use the render delta only as a review overlay or visual comparison.
