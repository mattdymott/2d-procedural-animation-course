Optional workshop · gait policy · about 2 hours

Design an Insect Gait

Add cadence without letting a group rule erase per-leg foothold legality or a recovery outcome.

Outcome

A six-legged creature alternates tripods, switches safely to a wave gait, and holds or emits recovery when one foot has nowhere legal to land.

Retrieval gate

Answer before looking: does cadence decide a landing point? No. It permits a group or leg to ask; that leg still owns its candidate and commits only at landing.

1

Gate with a planted base

Use alternating diagonal tripods while the opposing base remains planted.

2

Advance one explicit cursor

For a wave gait, advance only after the selected leg lands. Do not silently skip a bad candidate.

3

Change cadence safely

Use requested, pending, and active cadence with hysteresis; preserve plants and wait for an active-swing boundary.

Transfer rep in Unity

  1. Overlay tripod group, phase, and each foot’s candidate/plant state.
  2. Block one selected landing: the active group must hold or request recovery without waking the base.
  3. Request a wave/tripod switch while a leg swings; show that it occurs only at a safe synchronization point.
