Optional workshop · diagnostic practice · about 60 minutes

Debug a Gait Contract

Diagnose the writer that caused a slipping plant or chasing target instead of hiding its symptom with IK or sprite offsets.

Outcome

You can inspect a gait in authority order, reproduce two common state-contract failures, and name the sole legal transition that repairs each one.

Retrieval gate

Say the two rules from memory: a plant changes at landing; a candidate changes at permitted selection. Everything else is derived, observed, or visualized.

1

Reveal plant dragging

Turn a creature with world-pinned feet, then enable the body-dragged bug and watch honest stress disappear.

2

Reveal target chasing

Reserve a foothold, turn during swing, then enable re-querying to catch the candidate changing.

3

Trace ownership

Draw resolved pose, homes, plants, candidates, gait permission, and only then IK/presentation.

Transfer rep in Unity

  1. Pause on a sharp turn and expose each writer in authority order.
  2. Inject a plant-dragging bug and a candidate-requery bug behind separate toggles.
  3. For each bug, identify the illegal writer and the one legal state transition that may update the value.
