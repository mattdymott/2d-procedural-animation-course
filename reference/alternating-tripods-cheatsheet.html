Alternating Tripods Cheatsheet

A six-legged top-down gait policy, taught in Lesson 22.

The groups

Tripod A: front-left, middle-right, rear-left
Tripod B: front-right, middle-left, rear-right

A may begin a coordinated swing only while B is planted.
B may begin a coordinated swing only while A is planted.

Tripods are an authored cadence policy. They decide which legs may request a swing; each leg still needs its own legal planar target and retains its own swing state.

Group start

groupUrgency = max stress among planted group legs
if (opposingTripodIsPlanted && groupUrgency > threshold):
    for each leg in chosen tripod:
        candidate = planarQuery at this swing start
        if candidate is legal: begin that leg's swing
        else: hold or use recovery policy

Rules to keep

RuleWhy
One planted tripod supports the other.The base remains broad and easy to read.
Each leg filters its own candidate.Group rhythm never creates impossible promises.
Support failure holds or recovers.Never launch the opposing tripod to hide a bad target.
Presentation only decorates swing.Raised sprites never change phase or target.
