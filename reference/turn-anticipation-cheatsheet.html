Top-Down Turn Anticipation Cheatsheet

A render-only cue before a planned turn, taught in Lesson 27.

The contract

Locomotion publishes intended turn direction. Presentation may use it to pose a short opposite visual bank; it never changes heading, targets, or gait timing.

anticipationTarget = -sign(requestedTurn) * visual.AnticipationBank
visual.Bank = UpdateSecondOrder(visual.Bank, anticipationTarget, dt)

// When resolved turn begins, filter naturally heads toward resolved bank.
// Simulation continues to own heading, plants, and collision.
