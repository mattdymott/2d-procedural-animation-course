Non-negotiable contract

A planted foot is a persistent world-space promise. A heading-relative home is a freshly derived preference. Stress is the distance between them.

homeWorld = bodyWorld + Rotate(localHome, resolvedHeading)
stress = distance(plant.WorldPoint, homeWorld)

// Only a completed swing may replace the promise.
plant.WorldPoint = candidate.WorldPoint

Repair rule

Do not hide a sliding plant with IK, a shadow, a sprite offset, or a more frequent raycast. Find the writer that changed a world promise outside the completed-swing transition.
