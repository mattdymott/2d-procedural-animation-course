Muscles Cheatsheet

The three muscle recipes for verlet bodies. Taught in Lesson 2; the underlying partial-correction trick is Jakobsen §5 (springs). Foundations: verlet cheatsheet.

The one idea

Muscle = a constraint corrected only fractionally. Bones correct 100% of error per sweep; a muscle corrects strength ∈ (0,1) of the distance to a wish. Fire all muscles once per fixed tick, then run the hard constraint sweeps — muscles propose, constraints dispose.

Tick order

1. integrate all points          (verlet + gravity + damping)
2. fire muscles once             (rhythm → goal → tone, all partial)
3. legalize                      (k hard distance sweeps + pins)
4. collide/clamp

Layering rules

  • Superposition is free — all muscles are just position nudges; they add. Life = goal + rhythm + tone running simultaneously.
  • Envelopes prevent turf wars. Two muscles shoving the same points fight (visible as jitter or orbiting around the target). Fade each muscle out where another needs authority.
  • Strengths are per-fixed-tick fractions — meaningless without a fixed timestep. In DOTS: FixedStepSimulationSystemGroup, always.
  • Variation is data. Randomize tone/speed ±15% per creature at spawn to kill the clone-army look.
