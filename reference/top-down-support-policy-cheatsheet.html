Top-Down Support Policy Cheatsheet

Coordinating steps during turns, taught in Lesson 18.

The rule

A leg may swing only when its target is urgent and the remaining planted feet still form a believable base. This is a gait policy, not a hidden physics simulation.

Eligibility

urgency = length(plant - headingRelativeHome)

mayLift(leg) =
    leg is planted
    && urgency > comfort
    && remainingPlantedCount >= minSupportCount
    && RetainsSupport(bodyPosition, remainingPlants)
    && policyAllows(leg, gaitPhase)

Choose among eligible legs

StepAction
1. GatherRead every leg’s plant, state, heading-relative home, and stress.
2. RejectReject legs whose lift would leave too few or badly arranged planted feet.
3. RankPrefer greatest stress, optionally biased by turn direction or authored gait phase.
4. CommitBegin one swing; hold the rest until a support leg lands.

Choose the simplest policy that reads well

CreatureGood first policyWhen to deepen
BipedOther foot must be planted.Only if quick pivots look wrong.
QuadrupedDiagonal partner guard or at least three planted feet.When turning produces a stiff shuffle.
Six+ legsMinimum planted count plus alternating groups.When a static count permits an implausible cluster.

Failure-safe behaviour

If every stressed leg fails the support test, hold, slow the intended turn, or use an explicit recovery step. Never start a second swing merely because a first one is taking too long.
