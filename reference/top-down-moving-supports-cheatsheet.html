Top-Down Moving Supports Cheatsheet

Moving walkways, turntables, and material-relative plants on a top-down movement plane, taught in Lesson 21.

The relation

// Commit at landing.
support = hit.SupportEntity;
localPlant = InversePlanarTransform(support, hit.Point);
surfaceOffset = 0;

// Evaluate while planted.
surfaceOffset += beltVelocityLocal * dt;
localPoint = localPlant + surfaceOffset;
worldPlant = PlanarTransformPoint(support, localPoint);

// At liftoff.
carry = VelocityAtSupportPoint(support, localPoint)
      + PlanarTransformDirection(support, beltVelocityLocal);

Support pose moves coordinates. Surface offset moves material through those coordinates. Gait decides planting and release.

State meanings

StatePurpose
Support entityNames the coordinate frame that a plant is relative to.
Local plantThe material point selected at landing.
Surface offsetAccumulated local travel for a belt/tread; zero for an ordinary platform.
World plantThe current planar IK/gait target derived from the relation.
Carry velocityContinuity supplied to planar locomotion at liftoff.

Planar turntable velocity

r = worldPlant - pivotWorld
pointVelocity = linearVelocity + angularVelocityRad * perp(r)
carry = pointVelocity + transformedSurfaceVelocity

Evaluate velocity at the committed contact point: a stationary turntable centre may have zero velocity while its rim does not.
