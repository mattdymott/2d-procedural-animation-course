Gait Recovery Cheatsheet

Handling a permitted leg with no legal planar foothold, taught in Lesson 25.

The contract

An invalid foothold is a result, not permission to break cadence. Keep the current plant, hold the policy cursor/group, and request an explicit recovery.

candidate = FindLegalCandidate(permittedLeg)

if (!candidate.Exists):
    gait.Recovery = HoldingForFoothold
    locomotion.RequestedSpeed = 0 // or authored safe reduction
    locomotion.RecoveryDirection = ChooseSafeTurn()
    // do not advance cursor, switch tripod, or rewrite Plant
else:
    BeginSwing(permittedLeg, candidate.Point)

Recovery ladder

FirstThenNever
Hold current plant and reduce intent.Turn/backtrack with an explicit locomotion request.Teleport to a blocked point.
Retest at the next permitted swing start.Enter an authored stuck/escape state after a timeout.Advance a wave cursor silently.
Preserve support relations and phase.Resume only with a legal candidate.Move a plant in presentation.

Ownership

Gait detects the failed promise and owns recovery state. Locomotion consumes a semantic recovery request. The query adapter still reports facts; IK and presentation remain downstream readers.
