Stop and Go Weight Shift Cheatsheet

Render-only weight shifts around acceleration and braking, taught in Lesson 28.

The contract

Resolved planar acceleration drives a filtered visual offset. Locomotion, gait, and collision continue to own their original physical state.

acceleration = (resolvedVelocity - previousVelocity) / dt
shiftTarget = -normalize(acceleration) * visual.WeightShiftDistance
visual.Offset = UpdateSecondOrder(visual.Offset, shiftTarget, dt)

render.Position = Project(resolvedBodyPosition) + visual.Offset

Readable phases

PhaseVisual cuePhysical owner unchanged
BrakeBody drifts forward briefly.Locomotion stops; gait preserves plants.
PauseOffset settles over planted base.Support/cadence policy remains active.
AccelerateBody trails backward then catches up.Locomotion chooses speed; gait steps normally.

Boundary test

Toggle the shift off. Stop distance, heading, candidate choice, plant locations, and collision must remain exactly the same.
