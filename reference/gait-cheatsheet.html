Gait Cheatsheet

Event-driven stepping for IK legs. Taught in Lesson 4; composes two-bone IK with a two-state machine per foot.

The state machine

PLANTED  — IK target = plant point (a kept promise; body moves past it)
SWINGING — IK target = arc from old plant to new (fixed duration)

PLANTED → SWINGING:  stress > comfort  AND  partner is planted
SWINGING → PLANTED:  swing timer reaches 1

The three rules

RuleFormulaTuning
Trigger stress = |plant − home|, step when > comfort, partner planted Comfort ≲ half of max ground reach. Partner guard = whole coordination system; alternation is emergent
Target target = home + bodyVel · lead, clamped to reach annulus lead 0.1–0.3s. Zero lead → lands already stressed → limping. The highest-value dial
Swing x: lerp(from, to, smoothstep(t)) · y: ground − h·4t(1−t) Fixed duration 0.1–0.4s. Parabola lands soft automatically. Faster body ⇒ more steps, not faster swings

What emerges (never author these)

  • Step frequency and stride length — consequences of speed × the three dials.
  • Alternation — from the partner guard alone (quadrupeds: partner = diagonal pair).
  • Idle shuffling, turnaround steps — the trigger fires whenever circumstances demand.

Extensions

  • Uneven ground: home = raycast down from hip; plant on the hit point.
  • Phase-driven alternative: pin feet on a per-leg sine metronome (offset π) — regular, confident gaits; see Little Polygon loco series. Event-driven reads reactive/animal; pick per creature personality.
  • Full creature tick order: body/verlet + muscles → gait writes IK targets → IK solve → feet pinned into verlet buffer → hard sweeps.
