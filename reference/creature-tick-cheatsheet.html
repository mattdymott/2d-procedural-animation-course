Creature Tick Cheatsheet

The end-to-end walking-lizard hand-off, taught in Lesson 9. The creature is a composed package API; the tick tells each component who is authoritative, and when.

The invariant

Wishes first. Promises next. Legality last. Presentation never writes back. This lets every subsystem stay deep: it writes only the state it owns and consumes the result it is meant to see.

One fixed tick

StageWritesWhy it belongs here
1. IntentDesired root motion / steeringGameplay says where the creature wants to go; it does not pose bones.
2. Body wishesVerlet points, muscle proposalsIntegrate the previous state, then make partial rhythm/tone corrections.
3. Gait promisesFoot targetsRead current hips and body velocity; decide planted versus swinging feet.
4. IKKnee and ankle positionsSolve each leg against the target gait authored. Clamp reachability here.
5. Hard resolveFinal legal body / pinsRelax distance constraints and feet pins. This is the last simulation authority.
6. PresentationRender transform, sprite / mesh parametersRead the resolved pose; second-order juice may lag or stretch the picture, never physics.

Debug in hand-off order

  1. Freeze presentation. Is the resolved simulation legal?
  2. Freeze gait. Do body points remain healthy with their existing foot targets?
  3. Draw intent, foot targets, and pins in different colours. Which owner last wrote the wrong value?
  4. Only then tune values. A wrong authority boundary cannot be fixed with a slider.
