Top-Down Body Language Cheatsheet

Filtered visual bank and stretch from resolved planar motion, taught in Lesson 26.

The contract

Presentation reads resolved heading, speed, and turn rate. It writes only render-facing bank, stretch, and offsets.

turnRate = SignedAngle(previousForward, resolvedForward) / dt
bankTarget = clamp(-turnRate * bankGain, -maxBank, maxBank)
bank = SecondOrderFilter(bank, bankTarget)

stretch = 1 + resolvedSpeed * stretchGain
render.rotation = heading + bank
render.scale = float2(stretch, 1 / stretch)

Inputs and outputs

Read from simulationWrite for presentation
Resolved body position and forwardRender rotation/bank
Resolved speed and turn rateStretch/squash
Current swing stateOptional body offset/follow-through
Planted feet and collisionNothing — presentation must not write them.

Debug rule

Disable the filter. The creature should lose character but retain legal plants, gait cadence, and collision. If it changes behaviour, presentation crossed the authority boundary.
