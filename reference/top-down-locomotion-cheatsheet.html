Top-Down Locomotion Cheatsheet

A planar adaptation of Lesson 4. Feet still make promises; “ground” is now the movement plane.

The coordinate contract

plane:    XY (or XZ — choose once)
forward:  normalize(velocity, else previousForward)
right:    perpendicular(forward)

home[leg] = bodyPosition
          + forward * localHome[leg].x
          + right   * localHome[leg].y

Each leg owns an authored, creature-local forward/lateral home. Rotate it by current heading every tick. This replaces “project a hip down onto a flat floor.”

Gait: unchanged promise, richer trigger

RuleTop-down form
Stresslength(plant − home); translation and turning both create it.
Targethome + velocity × lead; lead remains a planar vector.
SwingInterpolate across the plane; add visual lift only for readability.
CoordinationKeep a partner or support-polygon rule so every foot cannot swing at once.

Walkability is not a support normal

QuestionAnswering seam
Where may a foot land?Planar walkability/tile/nav query at swing start.
Where is a foot now?Its committed Plant or SwingTo.
May the body occupy this?Obstacle/contact constraint, not a rewrite of a plant.

Author these rules

  • Local foot offsets: forward and lateral homes for each leg.
  • Comfort radius: tolerated translation/turn error.
  • Turn policy: heading change that requires an eligible leg to replant.
  • Visual lift: draw-height or sorting cue, never a source of planar targets.
