Rotating Support Cheatsheet

Continuity when a planted foot leaves a rotating support, taught in Lesson 14.

The contract

Carry the velocity of the committed contact point, not the support centre. Resolve the current world plant first, then evaluate linear and angular motion there.

// world plant is already the IK target while planted
float2 r = leg.WorldPlant - support.PivotWorld;
float2 pointVelocity = support.LinearVelocity
                     + support.AngularVelocityRad * new float2(-r.y, r.x);

// Conveyor material, if any, remains a separate third term.
carryVelocity = pointVelocity
              + pose.TransformDirection(belt.VelocityLocal);
