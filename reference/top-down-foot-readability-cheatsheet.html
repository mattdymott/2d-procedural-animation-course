Top-Down Foot Readability Cheatsheet

Making a planar swing legible without changing its simulation target, taught in Lesson 19.

The three representations

RepresentationAuthorityPurpose
PlanarFootGait/IKThe committed target on the XY/XZ movement plane.
ShadowPointPresentation reads PlanarFootShows where the foot is above the ground plane.
VisualLiftPresentationScreen/draw offset that makes a swinging foot visible.

Visual recipe

t    = swingProgress
lift = visualStepHeight * 4 * t * (1 - t)

shadow.position = Project(planarFoot)
foot.position   = shadow.position + screenUp * lift
foot.sortKey    = SortFromGroundPoint(planarFoot) + swingBias

Never feed lift or a screen-space foot position back into gait, IK, collision, or a world query.

Sorting policy

  • Compute base sorting from the planar contact point, not a transient raised sprite coordinate.
  • Give a swinging foot an explicit semantic bias/layer if it must read over its own shadow or body.
  • Decide foliage/wall occlusion intentionally in the presentation adapter; it is a game-art rule, not a gait rule.
